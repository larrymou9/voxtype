How it works

Three steps, no windows to manage.

VoxType has no interface to open. It waits for a key.

01 / HOLD

Press ⌃⌥

Anywhere — any app, any text field. VoxType is always listening for the hotkey, never for your voice.

02 / SPEAK

Say it

A local Whisper model transcribes as you go, entirely in memory. Nothing is written to disk.

03 / RELEASE

It's there

Let go of the keys. Your words appear right where your cursor was — no copy, no switch, no wait.

Why local matters

Most dictation apps mail your voice to a server. This one doesn't.

Cloud dictation tools are convenient because someone else's GPU does the work — and charges you monthly for it. VoxType does the work on the machine already in front of you.
